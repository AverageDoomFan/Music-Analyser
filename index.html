Where to start?

I have my audio files

  1. Drop them (or a whole folder) anywhere on the page, or use Import.
  2. Library: scores, curves, key, mood; open a track to see everything.
  3. Tune it to how you feel: lyrics, duels, genres, games.
  4. Set: draw a curve, get an ordered playlist.

I have a Spotify playlist

  1. Spotify: log in (once) and import the playlist.
  2. Live: the app plays each track on your Spotify and analyses it in real time.
  3. Map, comparison, split, sorted playlist created on your account.

I want to see first

  1. Test bench: synthetic tracks that move a single variable, from min to max.
  2. Live › Demo mode: the live scan without Spotify.
  3. Test tracks also let you try Set, duels, games and genres.

    Test bench

    Synthetic tracks, made in your browser, that move a single variable at a time (min, mid, max) while the rest stays put, plus a few sweeps where a variable changes over time. They go through exactly the same analysis as your files. The grid compares what the algorithm measures with what is expected.

    They are caricatures: they check that every measure reacts in the right direction and over its whole range, not that the score is right on real music. Test tracks join the library, tagged “Test”, and also let you try the Set, duels, games, splitting or the Live tab's demo mode.

    Before starting (Windows)
    • Spotify › Settings › Playback: turn off Normalize volume (the “Loud” mode compresses the dynamics) and crossfade.
    • System audio: nothing else must play during the scan (videos, notifications). The sound must stay audible.
    • Silent scan: install VB-Cable, then Windows Settings › Sound › Volume mixer › Spotify › Output = “CABLE Input”. Here, pick the “CABLE Output” input.
    • The audio is never recorded: it is analysed in memory then discarded, only the measures are kept.
